Privacy Policy: Hamster to Hound Pet Shop: Build & Grow

Published by Veyquora Studio · Contact: veyquorastudio@gmail.com · Effective date: 11 November 2026

Hamster to Hound Pet Shop does not collect, store or share any information about you or your child.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect personal information, and does not use device or advertising identifiers, analytics, crash reporting, location, the camera or the microphone. There are no accounts, no chat, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no links to websites or other apps.

Internet

The app requests no Android permissions, not even internet access, and never connects to the internet. Everything it needs is inside the app.

Saved progress

Your shop's progress and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are excluded from cloud backup. On newer Android versions, a device-to-device transfer that you start yourself can copy them to your new device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.

Children

The game is designed for children. It collects no personal information from children or from anyone else.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party services, advertising or analytics code. It includes an open-source graphics library (three.js) and two fonts (Bagel Fat One and Lexend) as files inside the app; none of them connect to the internet.

The app is distributed by the Amazon Appstore, which Amazon operates under its own privacy notice. Amazon may give us anonymous, combined statistics such as download counts; these do not identify anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
